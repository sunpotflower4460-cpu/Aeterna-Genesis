3. Ideal material mixture v01: retained and excluded

Generic isothermal A+B <-> C plus conserved material composition phi, reciprocal
solubility interactions and ideal mixing. The single N24/L32/T400/seed17 pilot
conserves its building blocks and decreases its finite-grid free energy.

It nevertheless fails a more fundamental continuum check. Let fixed solute mass
m concentrate within radius R and choose phi=A/R f(x/R). The gradient, quartic
and attraction terms scale as
  [C2*kappa*A^2 + C4*beta*A^4 - C1*chi*m*A]/R.
For sufficiently small A>0 the coefficient is negative; ideal entropy increases
only logarithmically. A uniform O(R^2) compensation preserves the material mean.
The continuum F is therefore not bounded below. A finite grid can hide collapse.
The pilot and original source remain untouched, classified as unqualified.
See 20261108-reactive-mixture/MODEL_LIMIT.json. This is not a membrane finding.

4. Bounded reciprocal material v02: a separate law from t=0

Constitutive hypothesis, not fitted to identified molecules:
  F = integral[alpha phi^2/2 + beta phi^4/4 + kappa |grad phi|^2/2
      + sum_i c_i(log c_i-1+g_i) - chi phi n + eta n^2/2],
  n=a+b+2c; g_A=g_B=0; g_C=-log K; eta>0.
  phi_t = M lap(mu_phi); J_i=-D c_i grad(mu_i).
  A+B <-> C, R=.02*rate*(K*a*b-c).
F in the thermodynamic equations is an integral. The solver's free_energy CSV
column and plotted values are its spatial mean f=F/volume, at fixed box size.
Conservation/monotonicity tolerances apply to those recorded density values.
Every reaction conserves A and B building blocks; both reaction directions are
removed together in the frozen control. The nonideal chemical potentials cancel
from the reaction affinity by stoichiometry. No protection/degradation mask,
inside/outside labels, autocatalytic multiplication or growth rule is supplied.

Bound:
  eta n^2/2 - chi phi n = eta/2(n-chi phi/eta)^2 - chi^2 phi^2/(2eta).
Beta>0 bounds the remaining polynomial below; ideal mixing has a finite lower
bound. Thus v01's continuum-collapse argument does not apply to v02.

Defaults: alpha1, beta4, kappa2, eta.1, M1, D5 for all three species, chi1, K10,
rate1. All runs start with mean-zero .01 RMS material noise and spatially uniform
a=b=.5-c, c=(11-sqrt21)/20. Those initial complexes are PLACED. This is not the
origin of those chemicals. Phi is an unbounded composition deviation, not a
literal bounded solvent fraction or a bilayer thickness variable. RT=1; heat
flows to a thermal bath. Total microscopic energy is not simulated.
