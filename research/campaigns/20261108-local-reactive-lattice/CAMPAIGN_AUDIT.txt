Physical assumptions placed at t=0
Periodic cubic molecular lattice (spacing 1, kBT=1), single-site exclusion,
neutral macromolecules with I/B conformers, H=df*N_B+epsilon*N_BB. Pair contacts
are local and counted once; the Hamiltonian is bounded below at fixed molecule
count. Vacancy denotes implicit overdamped solvent; solvent momentum, atomistic
chemistry, finite fuel transport and hydrodynamic flow are not simulated.
All initial occupied sites are sampled uniformly without replacement at density
.18, with floor(M/2) randomly positioned B molecules. No droplet, membrane,
inner/outer label, target image, catalyst object or body shape is placed.

Passive B<->I and fuel-coupled B+F<->I+W are separately reversible. Their local
forward/reverse rate ratios are exp(-DeltaH) and exp(-DeltaH+mu), respectively.
The second saddle has declared contact energy epsilon_ts=ts_fraction*epsilon.
It is an assumed local molecular energy, not a measured catalyst or an
emergent protection property. No rate refers to an object or inside/outside.
F/W activities exp(+mu/2), exp(-mu/2) are uniform ideal external reservoirs,
constant throughout each trajectory. Both signs and zero drive are tested.

Work and medium entropy
Every net driven B->I transfers one F to W and chemical work mu. H is checked
independently from the lattice. The column heat is generalized medium entropy
flow times kBT, summed from local log forward/reverse rate ratios; the model's
identity is heat = work - (H-H0). This is not calorimetry for a named chemical
fuel. See THERMODYNAMIC_SCOPE_NOTE.json. No state is clipped or mass-repaired.

What happened, including alternatives
At original df1.2/epsilon-1.2, negative drive mu=-2 with the half-contact saddle
produces dense regions in all three N24 discovery seeds. The same qualitative
contrast appears in all three new N32 holdouts and in larger volume/longer time.
N32 late B fraction is about .874; final width-4 material variance .0654..0690.
At N48/T1000 there are TWO threshold-.5 regions (volumes7331 and6576), while
N32 has about one. A single final picture therefore cannot establish an
intrinsic stationary droplet size or arrest of coarsening.

Positive drive at the original df consumes more chemical work per molecule
than the negative-drive case but remains dilute. This rules out using positive
chemical work alone as a proxy for a vessel. It is not a new finding counted
merely because mu!=0 supplies work.

The common-saddle negative-drive control is exactly configurationally equivalent
at stationarity to passive df=.2: df_eff=df+log[(1+eta exp(mu/2))/(1+eta exp(-mu/2))].
Both controls produce dense material without needing fuel for that equilibrium
distribution. This is a strong known-law counterexample to attributing every
dense region to chemical maintenance. N32 B fractions are .828..832 for the
common saddle and .826..830 for passive df.2; density variance ranges overlap.
The half-contact driven case has a different B population, so these controls do
NOT composition-match it. A nonequilibrium-specific structural claim remains
unresolved, not promoted.

Two-time identity retention is modestly above the chance fraction of molecules
in a dense region, but these are liquid molecules exchanging conformation and
position. The readout is not continuous residence, selective permeability,
inheritance or a membrane test. Labels are computed after the run and never
enter the dynamics.
